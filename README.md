# EeveeImmersive

An immersive, Apple Music-inspired playback experience for EeveeSpotify on iOS.

EeveeImmersive is a customization project focused on bringing the visual elegance and immersive playback experience of Apple Music to Spotify through EeveeSpotify. By reimagining the way music is presented, the project aims to create a more cohesive, visually rich, and native-feeling listening experience across iOS.

Built around the idea that music deserves more than a standard playback interface, EeveeImmersive explores a design language centered on album artwork, fluid layouts, subtle visual details, and seamless integration with the iOS environment.

The goal is simple: combine the flexibility of Spotify with the distinctive aesthetic of Apple’s music player.

⸻

# ✨ Overview

Modern music players are about more than simply playing songs. Artwork, animations, colors, typography, and interface transitions all contribute to the listening experience.

EeveeImmersive aims to bring these elements together in a unified interface inspired by Apple Music, while retaining Spotify as the underlying music platform.

The project focuses on creating an immersive player that feels intentional, polished, and consistent with the design conventions of iOS.

# Key design goals

* Immersive playback — Give album artwork and the currently playing track a more prominent visual presence.
* Apple Music-inspired aesthetics — Draw inspiration from Apple’s approach to artwork, layouts, colors, and playback controls.
* Lock Screen experience — Aim for a more visually cohesive playback experience between Spotify and the iOS Lock Screen.
* Native-feeling interface — Prioritize visual consistency with iOS rather than introducing an entirely foreign design language.
* Attention to detail — Focus on spacing, proportions, artwork presentation, and the overall balance of playback controls.
* A cohesive experience — Bring the in-app player and Lock Screen closer together in visual style.

⸻

# 🎨 Design philosophy

EeveeImmersive is built around a simple principle: the interface should complement the music, not compete with it.

Apple Music provides a useful design reference for this approach. Its immersive playback presentation makes album artwork the centerpiece, using visual hierarchy and contextual colors to create a stronger connection between the interface and the music.

EeveeImmersive takes inspiration from these ideas, with an emphasis on:

* Large, visually prominent album artwork.
* Clean and balanced playback layouts.
* Artwork-driven visual presentation.
* Minimal unnecessary interface clutter.
* Consistent styling across playback surfaces.
* A refined appearance that complements the iOS experience.

The intention is not to replace Spotify’s identity entirely, but to explore an alternative presentation that feels more personal, atmospheric, and visually immersive.

⸻

# 🎵 In-App Player

The in-app player is a central part of the EeveeImmersive concept.

Rather than treating the currently playing track as a simple collection of text and controls, the design emphasizes the relationship between the song, its artwork, and the surrounding interface.

The intended experience prioritizes a visually engaging playback screen while preserving the familiarity of Spotify’s core listening workflow.

Potential areas of customization include artwork presentation, layout proportions, background treatments, and the visual relationship between track information and playback controls.

The ultimate objective is a player that feels spacious, modern, and focused on the music.

⸻

# 🔒 Lock Screen Integration

A consistent playback experience should extend beyond the application itself.

The iOS Lock Screen is an important part of everyday music listening, and its playback interface should feel like a natural continuation of the in-app player.

EeveeImmersive therefore places particular emphasis on the relationship between its visual direction and the system’s Now Playing interface.

The design goal is to achieve a more cohesive appearance between the in-app playback screen and the Lock Screen, with album artwork remaining central to the experience.

Note: The extent of Lock Screen customization depends on the implementation, iOS version, and the interfaces available to the tweak. Changes to the in-app player do not automatically guarantee equivalent changes to Apple’s system playback interface.

⸻

# 📱 Built for iOS

EeveeImmersive is intended for the iOS ecosystem and is designed around the visual conventions of Apple’s mobile operating system.

Its design direction takes inspiration from the platform’s use of layered interfaces, rounded elements, artwork-focused layouts, and carefully balanced visual hierarchy.

The project is associated with EeveeSpotify, allowing the customization concept to target a Spotify-based listening experience rather than requiring a separate music player.

Compatibility depends on the specific implementation, Spotify build, EeveeSpotify version, installation method, and iOS release.

# 🛠️ Project Goals

EeveeImmersive is an ongoing customization project with the following priorities:

1. Develop a distinctive, immersive playback aesthetic.
2. Explore an Apple Music-inspired presentation for Spotify.
3. Improve visual consistency between the in-app player and Lock Screen experience where technically possible.
4. Preserve the underlying Spotify listening experience.
5. Refine the interface through testing, iteration, and attention to detail.
6. Document installation requirements, supported versions, and known limitations as they become established.

The emphasis is on thoughtful customization rather than adding unnecessary features.
